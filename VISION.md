# GM Commerce Vision

## Mission

GM Commerce will help Gathering Moss get products online faster, prevent inventory mistakes, and maximize sales with minimal human intervention while keeping Phil and Crystal in control of important business decisions.

## Business Context

Gathering Moss is operated by two owners who need the leverage of a much larger company without adding a large payroll. The system should eliminate repetitive administrative work so owner time can be spent growing plants, creating products, serving customers, and making high-value decisions.

## Version 1 Outcome

A non-plant product can move from SKU creation and photography to a reviewed online listing with as few human touches as responsibly possible. Product identity and inventory state remain consistent across the workflow.

The target flow is:

1. Generate or identify a SKU.
2. Create or locate the matching photo folder.
3. Add product photos.
4. Detect that the product is ready for listing preparation.
5. Generate a complete listing draft.
6. Let an owner review consequential details.
7. Publish first to Shopify.
8. Record marketplace identifiers and listing status.
9. Prevent duplicate or conflicting inventory states.

## Human Decisions to Preserve

Owners retain control over decisions such as:

- which products to carry,
- final pricing strategy,
- discontinuing products,
- introducing product lines,
- and approving material changes to brand voice or business policy.

## Repetitive Work to Eliminate

GM Commerce should progressively automate:

- SKU and folder creation,
- photo organization,
- product-data lookup,
- title, description, and tag drafting,
- category and attribute assignment,
- listing publication,
- marketplace-ID storage,
- inventory synchronization,
- sold-state handling,
- and identification of listings requiring attention.

## Explicit Exclusions

HydraCore and HydraCloud are personal or future-business projects and are not part of GM Commerce.

GM Money may integrate later but is not required to deliver the first useful version of GM Commerce.
